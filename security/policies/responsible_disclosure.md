# Responsible Disclosure Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in Cerberus AI, please report it responsibly.

### Contact
- **Email**: security@cerberus-ai.com
- **PGP Key**: Available at https://cerberus-ai.com/.well-known/security.txt

### What to Include
1. Description of the vulnerability
2. Steps to reproduce
3. Potential impact assessment
4. Suggested remediation (if applicable)

### Our Commitment
- **24 hours**: Acknowledgment of your report
- **72 hours**: Initial assessment and triage
- **7 days**: Status update on remediation plan
- **30 days**: Target remediation timeline

### Scope
- All Cerberus AI production services
- All Cerberus AI API endpoints
- All Cerberus AI frontend applications

### Out of Scope
- Social engineering attacks
- Denial of service attacks
- Third-party services and integrations

### Recognition
We maintain a Hall of Fame for responsible security researchers who help us improve our platform security.
