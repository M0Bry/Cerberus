# Rules of Engagement Template

## 1. Authorization
The Client hereby authorizes Cerberus AI to perform security assessment activities against the assets defined in the approved Scope of Engagement.

## 2. Methodology
- Passive reconnaissance and OSINT collection
- Vulnerability scanning and validation
- Controlled proof-of-concept exploitation (non-destructive)
- Risk assessment and business impact analysis

## 3. Prohibited Actions
- Deletion or modification of production data
- Denial-of-service attacks
- Permanent compromise of infrastructure
- Retention of sensitive client information post-engagement
- Testing of out-of-scope systems

## 4. Client Obligations
- Provide accurate information
- Authorize requested assets
- Notify relevant stakeholders
- Maintain confidentiality of findings

## 5. Liability
Cerberus AI operates as a non-destructive security assessment platform. Neither party shall be liable for indirect damages.

## 6. Confidentiality
All engagement data is encrypted and securely stored. Data is purged after engagement completion.
